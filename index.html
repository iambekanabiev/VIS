What CDL license do I need to drive for VIS INC?
You'll need a valid Class A CDL. We work with experienced drivers and those newer to the road — just reach out and we'll discuss your specific situation.
How soon can I start after applying?
Our process is fast. Most drivers complete screening and are assigned their first load within 3–5 business days of submitting their application.
What states do VIS INC drivers run?
We operate across 48 continental US states. Based out of Aurora, IL, our drivers run both Midwest-heavy regional routes and full OTR long-haul lanes.
Is there 24/7 dispatch support available?
Yes. Our dispatch team is available around the clock to support drivers on the road, coordinate loads, and handle any issues that come up during transit.
What type of freight does VIS INC haul?
We primarily haul dry van freight — general commodities, retail goods, manufacturing materials, and more for businesses across the country.
How do I contact VIS INC about shipping?
Call us at (945) 400-6416 or email it@vistrans.net. You can also use the contact form below to describe your freight needs and we'll respond quickly.
